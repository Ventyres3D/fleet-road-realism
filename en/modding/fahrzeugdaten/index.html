    Vehicle data¶

    Taxes and road charges of the own registration (vehicle tax in Germany, Austria, Switzerland, France, Italy, Poland, Czechia, Slovakia, Hungary, Greece, Romania, Bulgaria, Slovenia, Croatia, Serbia, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Albania, Turkey, Russia, Lithuania, Latvia, Estonia and Kosovo, Swiss LSVA) need data about a vehicle. Most of it is read from the vehicle's own .sii files:

    engine .siipower (info[] "... (270@@kw@@)", otherwise from torque and torque_curve[]), displacement (volume), electric (type: electric)
    chassis .siinumber of axles (residual_travel[]), empty weight (truck: sum of kerb_weight[], trailer: trailer_mass)
    body .siimass (added to trailer_mass)

    What the game files do not contain (Euro class, permissible weights, tractor or rigid truck, kind of suspension) comes from small optional files that a truck or trailer mod can ship next to its own definitions. The same files also set the position of plates and signs, doubles (registration, splitting when loading, cables, coupling sounds, coupling guide, support legs), animated accessories, the deck and the trailer lengths of driver tours.

    All files are optional. A missing file or a missing line is not an error, the default value given for each entry under "Keys" is used.

    Where the files go¶

    Same folder and same name as the .sii the vehicle uses, only with .v3def:

    engine         /def/vehicle/truck/your_truck/engine/your_engine.sii
            -> /def/vehicle/truck/your_truck/engine/your_engine.v3def

    engine sound   (for sound mods, cold start knock only, see "ENGINE SOUND")
               next to the sound .sui the engine .sii pulls in via @include:
               /def/vehicle/truck/your_truck/engine/sound.sui
            -> /def/vehicle/truck/your_truck/engine/sound.v3def
               and/or next to the .soundref from sounds[] "engine|...":
               /def/vehicle/truck/your_truck/engine/sound_engine.soundref
            -> /def/vehicle/truck/your_truck/engine/sound_engine.v3def

    truck chassis  /def/vehicle/truck/your_truck/chassis/your_chassis.sii
            -> /def/vehicle/truck/your_truck/chassis/your_chassis.v3def

    trailer        /def/vehicle/trailer_owned/your_trailer/chassis/your_chassis.sii
            -> /def/vehicle/trailer_owned/your_trailer/chassis/your_chassis.v3def

    company trailer (job trailer, no chassis/ folder: the chassis is the .sii
whose name starts with "chassis")
               /def/vehicle/trailer/your_company_trailer/chassis_hook.sii
            -> /def/vehicle/trailer/your_company_trailer/chassis_hook.v3def

    accessory      /def/vehicle/trailer_owned/your_trailer/accessory/r_bumper/your_bumper.sii
            -> /def/vehicle/trailer_owned/your_trailer/accessory/r_bumper/your_bumper.v3def
               (rear bumpers, see "ACCESSORY REAR BUMPER" under "Keys")

    mirror         /def/vehicle/truck/your_truck/accessory/mirror/your_mirror.sii
            -> /def/vehicle/truck/your_truck/accessory/mirror/your_mirror.v3def
               (also s_mirror, f_mirror etc., see "ACCESSORY MIRROR" under "Keys")

    interior       /def/vehicle/truck/your_truck/interior/your_interior.sii
            -> /def/vehicle/truck/your_truck/interior/your_interior.v3def
               (sleeping, windshield curvature, see "ACCESSORY INTERIOR")

    cabin          /def/vehicle/truck/your_truck/cabin/your_cabin.sii
            -> /def/vehicle/truck/your_truck/cabin/your_cabin.v3def
               (washer nozzles, see "WASHER NOZZLES")

    The examples in the def/ folder of this package show the format.

    Format¶

    key: value
    • one entry per line, spaces or tabs around the colon are allowed
    • lines starting with # or // are comments, a # after the value too
    • weights: tonnes (values up to 100, e.g. 18.00) or kilograms (above 100, e.g. 18000)
    • decimal point or decimal comma (18.50 or 18,50), no thousands separator
    • emission: whole number 0-7
    • type: tractor, rigid or swapbody (truck); semitrailer, dolly, drawbar, centeraxle or swapbody (trailer)
    • suspension: air or leaf
    • registration: own, next, prev or none
    • loading: own, next or prev
    • load_split: true or false
    • cable_connect: metres above 0 up to 20 (e.g. 1.2)
    • fixed_plate: game token of the country, e.g. germany, optionally |code
    • cylinders: whole number 1-16
    • knock_strength: number 0-3 (e.g. 0.5)
    • knock_tone: number 0.4-2 (e.g. 0.7)
    • mirror_axes: horizontal, vertical or both
    • mirror_rain_size: number above 0 up to 20 (e.g. 0.5)
    • sleeper: true or false
    • bed_head: a or b
    • washer: fixed or wiper
    • washer_jets: single or double
    • washer_jets_angle: degrees 5-170
    • coordinates (bed_area, washer_spot[], washer[n]): x;y;z as ZModeler shows them
    • italy_/serbia_card_shift_front / _rear: millimetres, positive = up, negative = down, -200 to 200 (e.g. 15 or -7.5)
    • italy_/serbia_card_depth_front / _rear: millimetres, positive = away from the vehicle, negative = towards the vehicle, -200 to 200
    • italy_/serbia_card_tilt_rear: degrees, positive = top edge towards the vehicle, negative = top edge away from the vehicle, -45 to 45 (e.g. 12.5)
    • greece_export_shift_front / _rear: millimetres, positive = up, negative = down, -200 to 200
    • greece_export_depth_front / _rear: millimetres, positive = away from the vehicle, negative = towards the vehicle, -200 to 200
    • greece_export_rotate_front / _rear: degrees, positive = top edge towards the vehicle, negative = top edge away from the vehicle, -45 to 45
    • plate_depth_front / _rear: millimetres, positive = away from the vehicle, negative = towards the vehicle, -200 to 200
    • plate_rotate_front / _rear: degrees, positive = top edge towards the vehicle, negative = top edge away from the vehicle, -45 to 45
    • nothing else on the line (no units like "t" or "kg")

    Keys¶

    Every entry starts with >>> and shows the line as it goes into the v3def (<...> = your value, | = one of them). Everything below it is description.

    ENGINE¶

    /def/vehicle/truck/xxxx/engine/xxxx.v3def

    General¶

    emission: 0 - 7¶

    Euro class 0-7

    Default: Euro 6

    COLD START KNOCK¶

    A cold diesel knocks: the mod adds a short metallic knock per ignition to the engine sound of the own truck, plus slightly uneven running and a harsher sound. How cold the engine is comes from the oil temperature exactly as the game computes it for cold_start_rpm (full up to about 19 deg C, gone from 90 deg C). Strongest at idle and when pulling away under load at low rpm, weaker with rising rpm. Electric engines do not knock. The engine sound comes from sounds[] "engine|..." of the engine .sii (also through @include), directly as "<bank>#<event>" or through a .soundref. Only this event gets the knock, not exhaust, turbo etc. Nothing has to be entered for it; the three lines below are only fine tuning.

    cylinders: <1 - 16>¶

    number of cylinders; sets the rate of the knocks (rpm / 60 x cylinders / 2 per second)

    Default: 6

    knock_strength: <0 - 3>¶

    strength of the knock (and of the uneven running) for this engine, 1 = normal, 0.5 = half as strong, 2 = double, 0 = off

    Default: 1

    knock_tone: <0.4 - 2>¶

    pitch of the knock for this engine, so it matches the engine sound; lower = deeper and duller (e.g. big V8), higher = brighter and harder

    Default: 1

    ENGINE SOUND¶

    /def/vehicle/truck/xxxx/engine/sound.v3def(next to the sound .sui)
    /def/vehicle/truck/xxxx/engine/sound_engine.v3def(next to the .soundref)

    For sound mods that only replace the sound .sui or only the .soundref: the same three lines as for the cold start knock of the engine, matching your own sound. The most specific value wins: .soundref .v3def before sound .v3def before engine .v3def. A missing line comes from the next file.

    cylinders: <1 - 16>¶

    as for the engine

    Default: value of the engine .v3def

    knock_strength: <0 - 3>¶

    as for the engine

    Default: value of the engine .v3def

    knock_tone: <0.4 - 2>¶

    as for the engine

    Default: value of the engine .v3def

    TRUCK CHASSIS¶

    /def/vehicle/truck/xxxx/chassis/xxxx.v3def

    General¶

    type: tractor | rigid | swapbody¶

    tractor = tractor unit for semi-trailers
    rigid = rigid truck (load on its own frame)
    swapbody = swap body carrier (chassis for swap bodies); tax and papers as rigid, in the German registration certificate „Wechselfahrgestell“

    Which trailers fit (fixed combinations, freight jobs with a company trailer, quick jobs): what counts is the type of the front part of the trailer (see TRAILER CHASSIS)

    tractor   semitrailer only
rigid     dolly, drawbar, centeraxle
swapbody  dolly, drawbar, centeraxle and swapbody

    Jobs that do not fit are only hidden for this truck, the offer stays.

    Default: tractor

    fixed_plate: "<country>" | "<country>|<code>"¶

    plates fixed in the model (without the game's plate locator), e.g. for packs with real, fixed plates on the vehicle

    fixed_plate: "germany"
fixed_plate: "germany|HN"

    The vehicle is permanently registered in this country from the purchase:
no temporary plate, no registration duty. Vehicle tax by the rules of
this country (weight, axles, Euro class etc. from the other entries, as
for any vehicle). Insurance is the statutory liability insurance,
changeable in the insurance menu (F7). The registration service is locked
for the vehicle: no registration tile in the service shop, no click on
the plate in the manager.
<country> = game token of the country (germany, austria, netherlands ...).
<code> = registration district as shown in the registration window;
without code the default district of the country. Matters where the tax
depends on the district (e.g. Poland, Romania, France).
Vehicles already owned (temporary, deregistered or registered in another
country) are re-registered to the country once; the old tax ends, no-claims
class and insurance stay.
Default:  no entry = normal registration
    gross_combination_weight: <tonnes or kg>¶

    permissible gross combination weight

    Default: 40 t

    plate_depth_front: <mm>¶

    front plate forward/back, mm

    Works like the cardboard keys, but for all normal plates of the chassis
(permanent and temporary plates; the cardboard signs and papers keep their own
keys). plate_depth_front / plate_depth_rear move the plate forward or back in
millimetres: positive values away from the vehicle (e.g. out of a recess or a
bumper), negative values towards the vehicle. plate_rotate_front /
plate_rotate_rear tilt the plate around its horizontal centre line (seen from
the side), in degrees: positive values move the top edge towards the vehicle
and the bottom edge away from it, negative values the other way round. A
trailer only has the rear plate. Only the vehicles of your company are moved.

      plate_depth_rear: 12        # rear plate 12 mm further out
  plate_rotate_front: -8      # front plate, top edge tilted 8 degrees away
Default:  0
    plate_rotate_front: <degrees>¶

    front plate tilt, degrees

    Default: 0

    italy_card_shift_front: <mm>¶

    Italian cardboard plate (foglio di via) front up/down, mm

    The Italian temporary registration (foglio di via, cardboard 275 x 200 mm) and the Serbian test plate (PROBA, cardboard 340 x 180 mm) are cardboard signs that are much larger than a plate. Depending on the model they can reach up to a rear light, be hidden behind an underrun guard or disappear into the plate recess. Italy uses the italy_card_ keys, Serbia the serbia_card_ keys. The Montenegrin test plate (PROBA) has the same size as the Serbian one and also uses the serbia_card_* keys, so one setting fits both.

    The default height already fits the vehicles of the game:

    truck frontcentred on the plate
    truck rear30 mm lower than the plate
    trailer10 mm lower than the plate

    ..._card_shift_front / ..._card_shift_rear move the sign up or down from this default for one chassis, in millimetres: positive values up, negative values down. ..._card_depth_front / ..._card_depth_rear move it forward or back: positive values away from the vehicle (out of the plate recess), negative values towards the vehicle. ..._card_tilt_rear tilts the rear sign around its horizontal centre line (seen from the side), in degrees: positive values move the top edge towards the vehicle and the bottom edge away from it (e.g. to follow an inclined plate holder), negative values the other way round. A trailer only has the rear sign.

      italy_card_shift_rear: 15      # 15 mm higher than the default
  italy_card_shift_front: -10    # 10 mm lower than the default
  serbia_card_depth_rear: 20     # 20 mm further out of the plate recess
  serbia_card_tilt_rear: 10      # top edge tilted 10 degrees towards the vehicle
Default:  0
    italy_card_depth_front: <mm>¶

    Italian cardboard plate front forward/back, mm

    Default: 0

    serbia_card_shift_front: <mm>¶

    Serbian/Montenegrin cardboard plate (PROBA) front up/down, mm

    Default: 0

    serbia_card_depth_front: <mm>¶

    PROBA plate front forward/back, mm

    Default: 0

    greece_export_shift_front: <mm>¶

    Greek export plate front up/down, mm

    The Greek export plate is smaller and higher than a normal plate (370 x 120 mm instead of 520 x 110 mm) and is drawn as a plate of its own, centred on the normal plate. greece_export_shift_front / _rear move it up or down in millimetres (positive up), greece_export_depth_front / _rear forward or back (positive away from the vehicle), greece_export_rotate_front / _rear tilt it around its horizontal centre line in degrees (positive = top edge towards the vehicle). Without an entry all values are 0. A trailer only has the rear plate.

      greece_export_shift_rear: 12     # 12 mm higher
  greece_export_rotate_front: -5   # top edge tilted 5 degrees away
Default:  0
    greece_export_depth_front: <mm>¶

    Greek export plate front forward/back, mm

    Default: 0

    greece_export_rotate_front: <degrees>¶

    Greek export plate front tilt, degrees

    Default: 0

    model_override: "<path>.pmd"¶

    own chassis model (.pmd), instead of model (and detail_model) from the chassis .sii

    Default: model of the game

    collision_override: "<path>.pmc"¶

    own collision (.pmc), instead of collision from the chassis .sii

    Default: collision of the game

    chassis_use: "<path>.sii"¶

    with the mod, this chassis runs as the chassis named here

    • the save game keeps this chassis - without the mod the truck drives as before

    registration: own | next | prev | none¶

    own = own registration next = belongs to the part behind it prev = belongs to the part in front of it none = no registration (e.g. swap body as a trailer with invisible

           wheels; automatic with "type: swapbody")

    "registration: none": the part is not a vehicle as far as registration goes, but a body or load:

    • no entry in the registration office, no registration tile in the service shop, no click on the plate in the trailer manager
    • no plates (no temporary plate when bought either), no vehicle papers
    • no vehicle tax, no insurance, no fines for registration

    The vehicle the swap body sits on stays registered as usual.

    Owned trailers with more than one chassis (B-double, HCT, semi-trailer on a converter dolly, full trailer with turntable) are a chain of parts in the game. Without a "registration" line every part is a vehicle of its own: own entry in the registration office, own plate, own tax and insurance. That is correct for semi-trailers and for converter dollies, which are registered vehicles of their own in reality.

    "registration: next" or "registration: prev" joins a chassis with its neighbour. Use it only for parts that are not a vehicle of their own in reality, e.g. the turntable axle (front part) of a full trailer with turntable steering. Joined parts are one vehicle:

    • one entry in the registration office, registered under the plate of the rearmost part (the one seen at the rear)
    • tax and insurance once, for the whole vehicle
    • no temporary plate of its own when bought; the joined part keeps the plate the game gives it

    "next" and "prev" are relative to the position, so the same chassis works in every configuration it is used in. Joins can be chained.

    Values of a joined vehicle
  axles          sum of the parts
  empty weight   sum of the parts
  gross weight   sum of gross_vehicle_weight of all parts
                 minus king_pin_load of every part behind the first one
                 (that load rests on the part in front of it and is already
                 counted there)
  king pin load  the one of the front part (load on the truck)

    Give every part the values it has on its own - nothing has to be split.

    Example 1: full trailer with turntable, 2 axles, 18 t (one vehicle)

    turntable axle (front)   type: drawbar
                         registration: next
                         gross_vehicle_weight: 9.00
                         (drawbar, nothing on the truck)
rear part                (no type = semitrailer, rests on the turntable)
                         gross_vehicle_weight: 18.00
                         king_pin_load: 9.00

    -> 1 vehicle, 2 axles, 9 + 18 - 9 = 18 t, no king pin load on the truck,
   fits rigid and swapbody

    Example 2: semi-trailer on a converter dolly (two vehicles, no "registration" line)

    converter dolly          type: dolly
                         gross_vehicle_weight: 18.00
semi-trailer             (no type = semitrailer)
                         gross_vehicle_weight: 39.00
                         king_pin_load: 12.00

    -> 2 vehicles with 2 registrations, each taxed with its own values
   (dolly 18 t, semi-trailer 39 t with 12 t on the dolly)

    Example 3: HCT semi-trailer + converter dolly + semi-trailer

    no "registration" lines-> 3 vehicles (semi-trailer, dolly, semi-trailer)
    dolly "type: dolly", semi-trailers without type-> fits tractor units only (front part semitrailer)

    loading: own | next | prev¶

    With the option "Split own doubles when loading" the chain of a trailer is spread over several loading lanes of the company: from the rear, every part gets a lane of its own and is detached there (key for detaching), the front part stays on the truck and is loaded on the lane of the game. After loading, the parts are coupled back together; the tour starts when the chain is complete. When unloading, the chain is only split if the hardest choice (most XP) is taken in the trailer delivery window.

    This works for own trailers and for company (job) trailers alike. It is only done when cargo is loaded or unloaded: simply dropping the trailer with the key for detaching (e.g. at a workshop or a trailer hub) always keeps the chain together.

of the chain:

  next  the link stays with the link behind it (e.g. a converter dolly stays
        under the rear semi-trailer)
  prev  the link stays with the link in front of it

    cable: prev | next | both | none¶

    who keeps the cable strand when split: prev, next, both or none

    When the chain of an own double is split (option "Split own doubles when loading", or a part detached with the key for detaching), the game itself has no rule for the cable strand between the two parts. The chassis .v3def decides:

    cable: prevthis part keeps the strand to the part in front of it
    cable: nextthis part keeps the strand to the part behind it
    cable: bothprev and next
    cable: nonethis part never keeps a strand

    Several "cable" lines are allowed. Between the front part A and the rear part B:

    1. B has prev or both-> the strand stays at B (rest position at B)
    2. otherwise A has next or both-> the strand stays at A (rest position as in the game)
    3. otherwise A has none-> the strand stays at B
    4. otherwise-> the strand is removed

    A strand that stays at the rear part keeps its trailer plug in the socket (e.g. of the dolly); the free end goes to a rest point of the chassis model:

    cable_rest_prev {
    rst_cable_y : 0.45; 1.20; 5.80 : 0; 0; 0
    rst_cable_w : 0.55; 1.20; 5.80 : 0; 0; 0
}
    name : x; y; z : heading; pitch; roll(metres, degrees, chassis model)

    The entry is chosen by the rest name of the cable accessory (upper / lower case does not matter); if the accessory has no rest name, by the ending of the socket locator (ele_cable_w -> ..._w), otherwise by the order of the strands. Missing rest points are listed in fleet_road_realism.log. When the parts are coupled again, the game cables the chain as usual and the rest strands disappear.

    How the DLL lays a rest strand:

    • The Z axis of the rest point (and of the socket locator) points to the end of the cable, i.e. to the plug head. The cable leaves on the opposite side: if the rest point points down, the cable goes up out of the plug.
    • Length: for straight cables straight_cable_length of the cable accessory, otherwise the rope length of the game.
    • At both ends the cable makes a bend (stiffness, the longer the cable, the wider); the extra length sags down in the middle.
    • The cable lies on the boxes of the chassis collision (.pmc, or collision_override): on a drawbar it lies on top. Boxes above both cable ends (frame, body) push the cable to the side or down, never over them.
    • If the cable is shorter than the way from the socket to the rest point, it is drawn taut - then move the rest point closer to the socket.
    Default:none (when split, the cable strand between the parts is removed)

    Models:

    • replacement models (interior and exterior): the previous mirror model without the glasses, with locators mglass_d, mglass_ds, mglass_p, mglass_ps, mglass_s, mglass_f, mglass_h instead (only the existing ones). Interior and exterior model use the same locator names. The locators get no hookup.
    • locator = pivot of the glass, rotation 0. On modern mirrors the adjustment motor or ball joint behind the glass, on mirrors whose whole head swings on the arm, the joint on the arm.
    • glass model (.pmd): origin in the pivot, the glass as it sits in the mirror model (only moved, not rotated). This position is the neutral position ("Reset" in the game). Interior and exterior have glass models of their own (different materials).
    • It turns by the angle of the game setting (sideways up to 10 degrees, up/down up to 7.5 degrees), in the axes of the camera locators. How far the glass moves follows from its distance to the pivot.
    • Exterior glasses are drawn on every vehicle with this mirror; only the own truck is adjusted, all others stay neutral.

    Errors¶

    A line that is present but cannot be read (e.g. "gross_vehicle_weight: 18 t", "emission: six", "emission: 9", "type: semi", "suspension: steel", "registration: dolly", "italy_card_shift_rear: 1,5 cm", "serbia_card_depth_rear: 2 cm", "italy_card_tilt_rear: 60", "plate_rotate_rear: 50") is reported as an error in game.log

    <ERROR> [fleet_road_realism] /def/.../your_chassis.v3def: invalid value for
        gross_vehicle_weight (expected e.g. "gross_vehicle_weight: 18.00"),
        using default 18 t

    and in fleet_road_realism.log. The default value is used for that line.
