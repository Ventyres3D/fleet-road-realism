    Splitting doubles and HCT¶

    With your own doubles and HCT combinations, every part goes onto its own bay for loading and unloading – just like in real life.

    Requirements¶

    • Setting: Fleet → Split own doubles when loading is on.
    • Your own combination: Only your own trailers are split. Trailers provided with freight jobs and driver jobs stay together.
    • The trailer must support splitting – the trailer's author decides this (Modding: vehicle data). Without it the combination stays together and loads as in the game.
    • Free bays: Every part needs a free bay at the company that it fits on. When loading, a part without a free loading bay gets a free unloading spot. When unloading, the combination stays together if there is no free bay for every part.
    • Unloading is only split if you choose the hardest parking option in the unloading window (German: „Ich beherrsche alles!“).
    • Waiting time: If the company has a waiting time, the bays are only assigned after it.

    Which part gets a bay?¶

    That depends on which parts receive or deliver cargo at this company:

    Cargo at this company What happens
    complete job (not assigned to a trailer) every part gets its own bay
    only for trailer 1 park trailer 2 somewhere (not on a bay), then take trailer 1 to the bay
    only for trailer 2 no splitting – the whole combination goes to trailer 2's bay
    several jobs are added together

    Loading¶

    1. Drive to the loading company – "Please drive to the loading lane" appears.
    2. Put the rear part on its bay. Once it stands correctly, the uncoupling hint appears.
    3. Uncouple – "Trailer parked".
    4. "Please drive to the next lane": park further parts the same way; the front part stays on the truck.
    5. Load the front part as usual – "Cargo loaded. Couple the parked trailers back on."
    6. Couple all parts again (see below). Then the tour starts.

    Unloading¶

    The same way: "Please drive to the unloading lane", park the parts, unload, then "Cargo unloaded. Couple the parked trailers back on."

    Coupling and uncoupling¶

    Normal doubles

    • Coupling: reverse up to the part (the coupling guide shows the position), hook in, then plug in the cables (with the key when advanced coupling is on, otherwise automatically). The landing gear goes up.
    • Uncoupling: unplug the cables, uncouple – the landing gear goes down.

    Doubles with cable distance (e.g. B-double – no room to plug in when coupled)

    • Coupling: reverse until "Reverse to connect the cables with [key] – remaining x m", plug in the cables, then reverse further and hook in.
    • Uncoupling: the key unlocks, landing gear down; drive forward at walking pace until "Disconnect the cables with [key]" and unplug. If you drive on, the cables come off by themselves.

    Parking trailer 2 on the road¶

    You can also park the rear part on the road, e.g. at a parking area while only trailer 1 has something to do. Conditions: the navigation points to a job or reservation for trailer 1, trailer 2 is still attached, the truck is standing, you are not at a company and no part stands on a traffic lane. Then the parking hint adds "Parking option: press [key] to drop trailer 2 here".

    The parked part has its own icon on the map. When nothing else is planned, "Drive to your parked trailer" appears.

    Container doubles¶

    • Loading: split only when an empty container sits on it.
    • Returning empty containers: stays together.
    • Unloading at a company with a stacker: never split.
    • Unloading at a company without a stacker: per trailer as above. If the front 20 ft container of a 2x20 sits on the rear part, park that part and drive the truck away so its front is clear.
