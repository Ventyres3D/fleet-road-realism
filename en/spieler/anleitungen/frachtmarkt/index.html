    Cargo market with your own trailer¶

    Requirements¶

    • Your own trailer on the truck.
    • A permanently registered vehicle (not on temporary/export plates, not deregistered).

    Partial loads¶

    • Not every cargo fills the trailer. As long as it is not full (space and permitted weight), the cargo market stays open and you can add more cargo.
    • A cargo fits only if
      • space, weight and dimensions are enough,
      • it is compatible with the load (temperature, food not with poisons, explosives not with other dangerous goods),
      • all deadlines of the trip can still be met,
      • sender and receiver lie within the radius around the parts already planned (road km; the first cargo is free). You set the radius under Trip planning: Radius for partial loads and separately Radius for partial loads (doubles, HCT, 2x20).
    • A company where you have already loaded does not offer a second time.
    • Each cargo pays its share of a full trailer.
    • Tanker, silo, tipper and container cargo always stays a full load.

    Job tiles and order¶

    • Up to 8 jobs at once. In the main menu every job gets a tile (up to 4 below each other, 5–8 next to them), sorted by urgency; the active one is highlighted.
    • The navigation automatically heads for the next destination that meets its deadline – if another job would then miss its deadline, that one comes first.
    • Decide yourself: click a tile, then the button "Drive here next". If your choice puts another deadline at risk, the mod warns you.
    • The HUD shows up to 4 jobs: the active one at the top, the next 3 below.

    Reserving¶

    • Reserving replaces the game's cargo market function for setting an offer as the navigation destination.
    • An offer you cannot load yet (e.g. because the company is elsewhere) can be reserved. It then already takes space, weight and deadline in the planning.
    • The navigation takes you to reserved cargo for pick-up; there "Load reservation": drive to the assigned bay and load.
    • Important with driving bans: reserved cargo that is exempt from the ban (e.g. on Sundays) already allows the empty run to the pick-up.
    • Cancelling a reservation costs nothing. If the offer disappears from the cargo market, the reservation lapses (message).

    Load list¶

    • When you are on the premises of a company, its cargo goes onto the load list (Add to load list / Remove from list). The radius does not apply here.
    • With doubles there is a load list per trailer (Load list trailer 1 / 2); cargo that fits on no single trailer only Add to load list (complete).
    • Everything on the load list is loaded in one go.

    Ready-made tours in the cargo market¶

    • Tour – one cargo, several receivers.
    • Mixed tour – several cargoes, several receivers.
    • Mixed load – several cargoes to one receiver.
    • Pay = sum of the single jobs. The deadline per stop includes driving time, loading time and driving/rest times.
    • Only as a whole: at the yard one loading, otherwise all parts are reserved.
    • After delivering part of a tour the receiver now and then asks "We would have cargo for …" – a return load to an open receiver of the tour or to the sender.

    Cancelling¶

    There is no "quickly cancel and be free". Whoever cancels pays the extra costs of a replacement carrier (cancellation costs, shown beforehand – the less time left until the deadline, the more expensive) and gets no pay for the tour.

    • Own trailer, not loaded yet: Cancel job on the tile – no costs.
    • Own trailer, loaded and left the yard: the cargo is handed over at the next suitable company – general cargo, flatbed and lowloader cargo at a logistics centre or forwarder, tanker, silo and tipper cargo at a company that accepts exactly this cargo; it must be open when you arrive. Choose "Hand over cargo" there, drive to the unloading bay and unload. Handling costs 8.50 € per unit. If there is no suitable company, the job continues.
    • Freight job (provided trailer): if the trailer is not coupled yet, the job is cancelled at once (with cancellation costs). If it is coupled, park it at the next parking area and unhitch it – a replacement carrier takes over. Continue delivery withdraws the cancellation.
    • Part of a tour: the cargo stays on and goes back to the sender; the tile turns grey. A click on the grey tile continues the job without costs.
