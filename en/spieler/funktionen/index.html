    Features¶

    An overview of everything Fleet & Road Realism does in the game. What can be switched on and off is listed under Settings.

    Number plates¶

    • Real number plates for every country in the game, in high resolution (detail level Normal, High or Ultra).
    • Foreign plates in AI traffic: overall share, share in cities, trailers and, if you like, separately for trucks, buses and cars. Police, taxis etc. always stay local.

    Registration, insurance and vehicle tax¶

    Guide: Registration and number plates · Insurance · Vehicle tax and charges

    Optional (setting Registration, insurance and vehicle tax). When off, you get random plates without insurance or tax, as in the game.

    • Purchase: The dealer fits every newly bought vehicle with a temporary plate for the country or city of the garage it goes to – included in the price. Expiry and validity abroad are announced; the HUD line shows the remaining days. The papers lie in the cab.
    • Registration: In the service shop (registration office → Re-register) you register or re-register the vehicle permanently: country, state or registration district and plate – as a random suggestion or with your own letters and digits following the real rules of the country. The country is preselected to the one you are in.
    • Deregistration: A deregistered vehicle has no plates any more; tax and insurance end. Driving deregistered or without liability insurance risks the fines of the country.
    • Insurance: Liability and comprehensive cover with no-claims classes, premium payments, downgrading after damage; cargo and job-trailer damage. On the road you manage it in the Personal settings window.
    • Vehicle tax and charges following the rules of the country of registration, with tax notice and refund; plus distance-based charges such as the Swiss LSVA. Tax keeps running while the vehicle stands in the garage.

    Fleet¶

    Guide: Garage relocation and vehicle transport · Fixed combinations and parking trailers · Splitting doubles and HCT

    • Garage relocation: If a vehicle may not drive itself (deregistered, temporary plate expired or not valid there), it is brought by a vehicle transport – with an invoice.
    • Fleet management: Fixed truck and trailer combinations.
    • Parking trailers: Unhitch your own trailers without a job outside company premises and leave them there; they are shown on the map.
    • Doubles and HCT: Split your own doubles over several loading bays; animated rear bumper; automatic height matching when coupling.
    • ALB: Load-sensing brakes for semi-trailers, trailers and your own truck – empty vehicles no longer lock up.

    Jobs and cargo market¶

    Guide: Cargo market with your own trailer · Opening hours and waiting times

    • Opening hours of companies: closed companies offer nothing.
    • Waiting times at companies with check-in and release.
    • Partial loads, mixed loads and tour planning in the cargo market with your own trailer; every cargo must fit by weight, volume and dimensions.
    • Pay for freight jobs like real haulage; surcharges per cargo.
    • Container traffic with reach stacker terminals and container chassis with up to 16 slots.

    Driver mode¶

    Guide: Driver mode

    The game's quick jobs have been rebuilt into a proper driver mode: you drive as an employed driver with the employer's truck and trailer.

    • Tour instead of single job: You choose the period you want. Cargo and destination show the first stop, the rest follows on the way – taking over cargo at a provided trailer, loading at the bay, return loads, empty runs.
    • Working time: Driver jobs are only available from Sunday 22:00 to Friday 18:00.
    • Pay: Wage and expenses, night bonus, ADR allowance. Deductions are paid by the employer.
    • Quitting: immediately (next parking area, engine off) or after the current job. No penalty, wage and expenses until the end.
    • Sign on permanently: At the end of a tour the employer asks whether you want to carry on. After a short tour the next one starts after 11 h rest, after a full week after 45 h weekly rest. The booked tour is shown in the box at the top left, where it can be cancelled. While a tour is booked, freight jobs, cargo market and subcontracting are locked.
    • Waiting times at companies can be bridged with a rest.

    Securing cargo¶

    Guide: Cargo securing

    Optional (Secure cargo yourself): twistlocks on containers, straps and chains – hook on, throw over, tension the ratchet –, edge guards, anti-slip mats. Unsecured or badly secured cargo can slide and tip. Click icons: red = still to do, orange/yellow = mouse over, green = done.

    Traffic rules¶

    Guide: Driving bans and night speed limits

    • Truck driving bans per country and city by weekday, time and weight, with exemptions for certain cargo and fines. A HUD notice appears while a ban applies.
    • Night speed limits for trucks with a notice at start, end and when entering a country.

    Vehicle and interior¶

    Guide: Vehicle

    • Mirrors: Switch each outside mirror between normal and manoeuvring position with a key; rain drops on the mirror glass.
    • Windscreen washer with jet, water and run-off; windscreen dirt (dust, insects, mud); washer fluid colour selectable.
    • Rain in the cab with the side window open.
    • Vehicle papers in the cab, place selectable.

    Performance¶

    Fleet & Road Realism automatically uses all cores of the processor. Heavy calculations such as routes, tour planning and partial loads, as well as writing the log file, run on their own cores so the game does not have to wait. On a single-core processor the mod calculates within the game itself.
